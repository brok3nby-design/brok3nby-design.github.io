About the studio

Strange games. Dark humor. Worlds worth exploring.

One creator. Plenty of strange ideas.

I'm the creator behind Brok3n by Design: a lifelong gamer, single dad, and full-time tech with a passion for making things. Each project is designed, directed, and built by me, from the first idea to the sound, worlds, and awkward jokes that make it feel alive.

I've been playing since the '80s. Years of image, video, and audio editing taught me how much a small detail can matter. That same curiosity now goes into games I want to explore, and keep coming back to.

Design. Build. Play. Refine.

I call it Flow Programming: build an idea, play it, question it, and keep shaping it until it feels right. Modern creative tools, including AI-assisted workflows, are part of that process. I choose the direction and keep refining the result.

I'd rather keep pushing a strange idea than settle for something forgettable. Rough edges are allowed. The feel has to earn its place.

Why “Brok3n by Design”?

“Flawless by Design” felt like a lie, and the domain was probably taken anyway. Brok3n is about being willing to take the work apart on purpose. Every version leaves something useful for the next one.

And the “3”? Every indie dev is contractually required to swap at least one vowel for a number. I don't make the rules. I just quietly resent them.
