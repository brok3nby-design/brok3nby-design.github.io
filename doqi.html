Overview

DOQI is a strange, funny, and slightly unsettling game about identity, choices, and how the smallest decisions can completely change who you are.

TL;DR: A satirical personality game where ridiculous choices quietly shape your identity and determine which ending you receive. A prototype currently on hold.
