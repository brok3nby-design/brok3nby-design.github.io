Overview

Start small. Find your place. Build something that lasts.

Speck brings exploration, gathering, crafting, progression, and social discovery into one persistent world. Set out across unfamiliar country, collect what you need, improve your tools, and create a home that remains part of the landscape for others to encounter.

The world is being designed to feel intimate up close and enormous beyond the horizon—somewhere that can grow from solitary journeys into neighborhoods, trading routes, towns, and shared stories.

Features

One Shared World

Explore alongside other players in a persistent place where meaningful changes survive and become part of its history.

Explore & Discover

Cross varied terrain, chart unfamiliar regions, discover settlements, and find reasons to venture beyond the known map.

Gather & Craft

Harvest resources, earn experience, craft stronger tools, manage equipment, and turn exploration into lasting progress.

A Place of Your Own

Establish a home, shape its interior, and gradually expand what you are able to build and protect.

Make It Personal

Choose how you present yourself and express your style freely, without making identity another grind.

Social Discovery

Meet other travelers, chat, leave messages, visit what others have built, and watch communities emerge naturally.
