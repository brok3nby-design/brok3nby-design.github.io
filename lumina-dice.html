Overview

Glass & Fortune is a dark tabletop roguelike built around five mutable glass dice. Build scoring hands across a series of Readings, then bend each roll with limited actions before you cast it. Every fortune asks how far you are willing to push your luck.

Bend the roll. Build the hand. Cheat fate.

Features

Glass Dice

Five dice carry a number, a colour, and a favour. Choose carefully before you turn, tint, or recast.

Loaded Hands

Build pairs, figures, spectrums, and stranger combinations, then cast the hand when the odds feel right.

Readings

Each Reading changes the table rules and raises the score you must reach before your casts run out.

Fortunes

Collect powerful effects that reshape how future hands score and how you approach the table.

Shards

Earn shards as your fortune unfolds and spend them to improve your chances in later Readings.

Save & Resume

Runs are automatically saved. Leave and continue later without losing progress.
