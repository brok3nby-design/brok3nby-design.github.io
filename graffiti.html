Graffiti

In development · Coming soon

Draw something small. Sign it. Leave it behind.

A living pixel-art guestbook for Brok3n by Design. Every visitor gets one tiny canvas, a name, and a permanent place on the wall.

Follow Development Back to Teasers

Overview

Draw a tiny piece of pixel art, add your name and a message, and leave something behind for the next visitor. Graffiti brings every contribution together in one shared guestbook, with a guided tour through the people and pictures on the wall.

Features

One Shared Wall

Every signature becomes part of the same public guestbook.

Live Count

See how many people have signed before you.

Guided Tour

Visit every artist and message one at a time.

How It Works

01 / DRAW

Build a Tiny Picture

Use a simple pixel studio to paint an icon, a face, a creature, or whatever fits.

02 / SIGN

Add Your Name

Leave a short signature and message beside your art, like a digital guestbook.

03 / EXPLORE

Tour the Whole Wall

See the total signer count, then move through every contribution one by one.

Latest Development

Coming soon. The drawing experience is built, but the public guestbook is not playable yet. Once the shared wall is ready, you will be able to draw, sign, and explore everyone’s contributions here.
