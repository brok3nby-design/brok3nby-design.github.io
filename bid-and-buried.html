The Locker Is Telling You Something

Hidden Archetypes

Hoarders, workshops, estates, dead shops, smuggler caches, and time capsules generate distinct kinds of lockers. Visible objects are clues to what lies behind them.

Excavation, Not Reveal

You cannot click straight through to the prize. Dig order matters, containers nest surprises, and bulky finds can reshape the entire haul.

The Right Buyer

Pawn Pete always offers quick cash. Rotating specialists pay far more for their categories, turning inventory into a wager on tomorrow.
