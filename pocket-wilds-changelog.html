POCKET WILDS

Update Log — by Brok3n By Design

TL;DR: A build-by-build record of Pocket Wilds updates, covering new content, art, polish, fixes and system changes. The main game page has the current high-level status.

BUILD 4-AUG-DV

AUG 4, 2026
  • THE ALLOCATION. Your landed dice are no longer just an attack — they are a hand to spend. Open ALLOCATE and tap each die onto a job: STRIKE for damage, GUARD to block that many pips of the next blow, FOCUS to recover its pips as stamina (8+ pips also build momentum).
  • Enough GUARD takes even a telegraphed mighty blow on the chin: no damage, and no shattered die. A third answer to the windup, alongside dodging and gambling.
  • Cracked dice are worth nothing on any job — but shunting your 1s out of the strike keeps them from stumbling it. The tray rewards the clever.
  • Fewer strike dice means a lighter blow, and a channelled cast now only breaks if your strike is heavy for your WHOLE pool — you cannot cheese it with one lucky die.
  • Spend everything on defense and breath if you like: no strike, no stamina cost, you simply set your stance for a turn.
  • The quick path is untouched: ENTER still strikes with everything, exactly as before. The LUNGE commits every die and never allocates.

BUILD 4-AUG-DT

AUG 4, 2026
  • The dice are yours to play now. When an attack roll lands, the tray WAITS: tap any dice to HOLD them, then REROLL the rest — once per throw — or STRIKE to take it as it lies.
  • Rerolling is a real gamble: every rethrown die can crack. Keep what is good, risk what is not, and read the stance before you decide.
  • Works with mouse, touch, or keyboard: number keys hold each die, R rerolls, ENTER strikes.

BUILD 4-AUG-DS

AUG 4, 2026
  • Battle enemies are ready to ACT their stances: drop in per-stance portrait art and the boss card swaps the moment it settles into a pose. Missing art falls back cleanly, so stances can arrive one image at a time.
  • The big stance word now rises in the gap between the two cards instead of over the enemy's face.

BUILD 4-AUG-DR

AUG 4, 2026
  • Bosses no longer act at random. Every boss ends its turn settled into a visible STANCE — shown under its health bar — and its next move is exactly what the stance promises.
  • PROWLING swings normally. WINDING UP is the big telegraphed blow: dodge it and the boss is left EXPOSED, or gamble a hit into the swing for +25%.
  • GUARDING halves your blows and blocks all momentum — dodge for stamina, drink, or set up instead of wasting a good roll. Your UNLEASH pierces straight through it.
  • CHANNELLING telegraphs its special. Land a HEAVY hit — a strong roll, a shield bash, a bomb — and the cast breaks, leaving it reeling and EXPOSED.
  • EXPOSED is never chosen and only earned: it takes +60% damage and can barely swing back. Dodges and cast-breaks buy you the window.
  • Hovering ATTACK now also shows what the stance will do to your damage before you commit, and the message strip prompts the counter-play.
  • Battle text greets bosses properly at last — "THE EMPEROR RAISES ITS GUARD", not "THE RAISES ITS GUARD".

BUILD 4-AUG-DQ

AUG 4, 2026
  • Battle dice now mean what they show. Damage used to be your roll divided by your own pool's average — so a bigger pool changed almost nothing, and five hard-earned bonus dice were worth less than one weapon level. Now every pip is worth a fixed slice of your weapon's power: a fresh hero hits exactly as before, but each die you forge, wear or take as a boon adds its full face value to the throw.
  • The damage ceiling is gone. A huge pool rolling hot now hits like it looks — the dice themselves are the only cap.
  • SHATTER finally has teeth: losing your biggest die now truly costs you its pips, not a rounding error.
  • Fair warning for veteran hoards: a fully-kitted hero now hits roughly twice as hard in the dice arena as before. The wilds may need to answer that in a future build.

BUILD 4-AUG-DP

AUG 4, 2026
  • A HOW TO PLAY board now stands in your yard, by the gate you leave through. Press E for a four-part guide: the wilds, dice battles, getting stronger, and home & hoard.
  • The vault's THIS HERO view finally means something. It counts everything your current hero has earned — what they are carrying AND everything they have already banked — instead of emptying itself the moment you made a deposit.
  • The other view is now labelled ALL TIME: every hero you have ever played, together.
  • The class-choice screen tells you your battle dice up front — the attack pool you throw, the d20 you dodge with, and what that handful is like to play.
  • The battle command reads ATTACK / ROLL, so it is clear that choosing it throws dice.

BUILD 3-AUG-DM

BUILD 3-AUG-DL

AUG 3, 2026
  • Every beast now moves like itself. Slimes surge and rest, rats dart, wolves circle, beetles charge, crabs sidle, snakes weave, golems grind forward and drakes swoop — with lean, bob, squash and stretch on top.
  • Monsters telegraph. A melee beast pulls back before it commits, so a hit can be read and stepped away from instead of simply happening on contact.
  • Ranged creatures charge a shot before releasing it, and the bolt now carries its caster's colour.
  • Slain beasts leave a brief tumbling death pose instead of blinking out of existence.
  • Tier, elite and champion creatures keep their own colours now. Instead of being drowned in a tint they wear what happened to them: scars, spikes, shoulder growths, glowing cracks and orbiting motes.
  • The wilds are inhabited, not sprinkled. Beasts arrive as colonies, packs, patrols and marching lines, and drift back toward the place they call home.
  • Species now trail their own ambient life — slime droplets, spores, dust, mist, embers and bone.
  • The four legendary wanderers are no longer recoloured copies of ordinary beasts. The Gilded Slime, Starfang, Voidmaw and the Bone Emperor are each drawn from scratch, in four frames.
  • Each legend moves by its own nature: the Gilded Slime hunts treasure, Starfang circles overhead, Voidmaw tears holes in space, and the Bone Emperor marches an ancient road. They announce themselves with their own sound, a reveal and a name — and ordinary beasts get out of their way.
  • Floating "!" and "?" markers are gone. Creatures signal with a ground flare and an eye glint instead.
  • Legendary wanderers, the Named Beasts and death poses all sort into the world properly now, so nothing draws through a tree.
  • Under the hood: the four motion beats now share their two painted frames instead of baking each one twice, so the richer animation costs no extra memory or loading time.

BUILD 1-AUG-DG

AUG 1, 2026
  • BIG FIX — bonus dice were making you WORSE. Stumbles and fumbles triggered on a flat count of 1s (2 and 3) no matter how many dice you threw, so every extra die a boon gave you raised your chance of failing. A loaded-up lancer was failing nearly half his attacks.
  • Those thresholds now scale with the size of your pool. Failure sits around 1-6% at every pool size, and collecting dice makes you MORE reliable, not less. Starting pools are unchanged.
  • The d20 uses the built-in die again, so every die in the pool matches.
