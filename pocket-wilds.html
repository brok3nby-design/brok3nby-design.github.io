Overview

In a kingdom long fallen, five beasts devoured the fragments of the royal crown and buried them deep in the wilds. You are a dreamer who followed the fragments into this overgrown, dangerous land.

Pocket Wilds is a complete, self-contained GBC-style action RPG built in a single HTML file. Explore a living pixel world that grows crueler the farther you roam. Earn each biome’s Lair Key through its horde, beast and camp trials, then descend into a boss fight that is not a menu but a dice pool — throw your class’s fistful, hold and reroll, and spend every die on striking, guarding or recovering while the boss telegraphs its next move through a visible stance. Forge your weapon stronger, bank a fortune in the Vault, and chase the shattered crown to one of several endings.

Features

Real-time Exploration

Classic top-down action across an endless procedural world of five concentric biomes — Meadowlands, Verdant Wilds, Howling Fringe, Ashen Reaches and the Forgotten Expanse. Smooth movement, dodge, block, knockback, i-frames and hitstop.

The Dice Battle System

Every boss fight is a dice pool, and each class throws its own fistful. Every pip is a fixed slice of weapon power, so each extra die adds its full face value — there is no damage ceiling.

Hold, Reroll, Allocate

After the throw, hold what you like and reroll the rest once. Then spend each landed die on STRIKE, GUARD or FOCUS. Natural 1s crack — but the thresholds scale with pool size, so collecting dice makes you more reliable, not less.

Boss Stances

Every boss ends its turn in a stance that promises its next move. WINDING UP is the heavy blow — dodge it or gamble into it. GUARDING halves your blows. CHANNELLING breaks on a hard hit. EXPOSED is never chosen, only earned.

The Dice Bag

A leather pouch on your kit. Every die you throw, named and sourced, your roll range and average, a working shake that throws them for real, and nine stones to cut your dice from — bone, ember, verdant, tide, royal, obsidian, frost, rose and gold.

16 Monster Species

Each with its own movement personality — surge, dart, circle, charge, sidle, weave, grind, swoop — plus telegraphed melee and charged ranged shots. Elite and champion variants wear what happened to them: scars, spikes, glowing cracks, orbiting motes.

Four Legendary Wanderers

The Gilded Slime, Starfang, Voidmaw and the Bone Emperor. Each drawn from scratch, each with its own roaming nature, reveal, taunt and rewards. Plus five named quarries found through beast-tracking.

Four Unique Classes

Knight, Lancer, Mystic and the unlockable Shade — each with its own weapon, special ability on cooldown, and its own dice pool. The knight throws 2d12+2d8; the mystic throws 2d20.

Levelling to 100

XP, six attributes and point-buy creation. Milestone level-ups deal you three cards, always including at least one boon — 53 boons and 6 class abilities per class, every one with painted card art.

Grand Delves

Twelve-room dungeons with locked boss vaults, jailers, levers, cracked walls, tablets and a great chest. Trapped chests and ancient shrines call for graded d20 checks against your own attributes.

The Homestead & the Vault

You wake at home and walk out through your own gate, past a forge, a workbench and decorations you bought and placed. Bank gold and gems into a clickable, jingling hoard that survives death and new games.

Camps, Not Menus

A fireside scene. The folk speak first and unprompted, your hero portrait listens opposite them, blinking bubbles track what is left to do, and the camp folk stand at their fire as painted figures out in the world.

The Greed Arc

Camp folk react to your wealth. The Vault whispers. And once your fortune is large enough, the Hollow Crown offers to match it coin for coin — leading to more than one ending.

Weapon Forging & Gear

Collect shards to upgrade your sword, spear or wand up to level 6 — forge level grants a bonus die all the way to a d20. Helms, armor, legwear and charms come in quality tiers, and your best piece grants a die of its own.

Achievements, Codex & Daily Wilds

40+ achievements, a living bestiary, a treasure catalog, a trophy hall, ten lore stones, a new seeded run every day, and a global leaderboard.

Cinematic Storytelling

46 painted story panels carry the boss reveals, the key ceremonies and every ending. Backed by 18 music tracks, 107 sound effects and 128 voice lines.

Crystal-clear Text

All in-game text renders on a native-resolution overlay, so words stay sharp while the world keeps its pixel scale.

Full Mobile Support

Touch joystick, action buttons, an interact button, a rotate gate, and layouts that hold together right down to 667×375.

Fully Self-Contained

One HTML file. No install, no dependencies, works offline. Save and continue, pause menu, fullscreen, display scaling, CRT filter and autosave.

Built and playable

The core loop

  • Endless procedural world across five concentric biomes, chunk-cached and seeded
  • Real-time overworld combat: three weapons at levels 1–6, a class special on cooldown, dodge, block, knockback, i-frames, hitstop, screen shake
  • 16 monster species, each with its own movement personality, plus elite and champion variants
  • Four legendary wanderers and five named quarries found through beast-tracking
  • Levelling to 100 with six attributes, point-buy creation and milestone card draws
  • 53 boons and 6 class abilities per class, every one with painted card art

The dice battle system

  • Per-class dice pools, with every pip a fixed slice of weapon power
  • Bonus dice from forge level, best gear, relics and your first three boon dice — each labelled by source
  • Cracked dice on natural 1s, with thresholds that scale with pool size
  • Hold and reroll once, by mouse, touch or keyboard
  • Allocation across STRIKE, GUARD and FOCUS, with momentum and UNLEASH
  • Five boss stances that telegraph the next move, plus SHATTER knocking your biggest die out of the fight
  • The Dice Bag: every die named and sourced, your range and average, a working shake, and nine stones to cut them from
  • Graded d20 exploration checks on trapped delve chests and ancient shrines

World and progression

  • The homestead: vault, forge, workbench, signpost, how-to board and placeable decorations
  • The Vault — a clickable, jingling hoard that survives death and new games, with wealth tiers and gem trophies
  • Treasure economy, a peddler who buys and sells, and fast travel between camps
  • Camps as fireside scenes where the folk speak first and unprompted
  • Horde, beast and camp trials gating each biome’s lair key — and the key can no longer be lost
  • Grand Delves: 12-room dungeons with locked boss vaults, jailers, levers, cracked walls and a great chest
  • The greed arc, the Hollow Crown’s offer, and multiple endings
  • Daily Wilds, free roam, leaderboard, 40+ achievements, codex, trophy hall, bestiary and ten lore stones

Presentation

  • Crystal-clear text on a native-resolution overlay, over a world that keeps its pixel scale
  • Painted art pipeline: hand-drawn heroes, per-species monster figures, painted boss portraits with per-stance swaps, and y-sorted world scenery
  • Ground tile variation — any tile accepts up to six looks, scattered tile by tile
  • 18 music tracks, 107 sound effects, 128 voice lines, 51 NPC lines, 60 vault lines, three-channel volume
  • 46 painted cutscene panels for boss reveals, key ceremonies and endings
  • Full mobile support down to 667×375, with touch joystick and a rotate gate
  • Save and continue, pause menu, fullscreen, display scaling, CRT filter, autosave

Still to come

Ordered by how much each one changes what you actually see. Every item below currently falls back to something that works.

The art pass

  • Painted hero sprite packs — 2 of 24 sheets. Eight heroes (four classes, male and female) × three sequences each. Any hero without a pack quietly uses the baked pixel figure.
  • Block animations — the third sequence, plus the small engine change to load it. Until then, blocking shows the painted idle and a ward shimmer.
  • Creature stance art — 7 of 14 creatures. Three named quarries and all four legendary wanderers still fall back to their base portrait, so battles read fine, the creatures just don’t act their stances.
  • World art — 1 of 5 biomes painted. Only the Meadowlands has its own trees, rocks, bushes, flowers and wonders. The outer four still use the built-in vector scenery, and need their own ground colours: dark forest floor, moor heath, black ash, packed snow.
  • Camp folk — 2 of 10. Brand and Old Maren stand at the Meadowlands fire; eight more to draw, two poses each.
  • Homestead yard art — the yard is still pixel placeholders. The Vault art is already done.
