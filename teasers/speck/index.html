Start from a template

Every design in Speck starts from a template. The template says what a thing is — a chair blocks the way, a rug can be walked over — and your pixels say what it looks like. Pick one, or pick one and start it empty.

How this works

Mouse
Left button paints with the current tool. Right button erases. Wheel zooms. Hold Space and drag, or use the middle button, to pan.
Touch
One finger paints. Two fingers pan and pinch to zoom, and a stroke that turns into a pinch is undone. Choose Move to pan with one finger.
Keyboard
Click or tab to the canvas. Arrows move the cursor, Enter/Space paints, Delete erases. P pencil, E eraser, F fill, K eyedropper, H move, M mirror, G grid, +/− zoom, 0 fit, Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo, 1–9 pick a swatch.
Saving
Your draft autosaves in this browser. Save editable design writes a .speck.json you can reopen here or import into the game's studio. PNG and JPG are pictures only.
Privacy
Everything happens on your device. Nothing you draw is uploaded.
