MOSSBACK THE ELDER

It remembers being small. It remembers being fed.

The pond beast guards the first lair. Read its stance, throw your dice, and spend them well — you are a fresh knight with a forged sword, one potion and one bomb.

  • Every turn Mossback shows its next move. WINDING UP is the big one — dodge it and it's left EXPOSED.
  • ATTACK / ROLL throws your whole fistful. Hold the good dice and reroll the rest once — but rerolled dice can crack.
  • ALLOCATE spends each die: STRIKE for damage, GUARD to block that many pips of the next blow, FOCUS for stamina.
  • Ones are cracked. Too many in one strike and you stumble, or fumble outright.
